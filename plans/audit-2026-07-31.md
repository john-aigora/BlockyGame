## 3. PERFORMANCE

### [P-1] ~700 shadow-eligible meshes, including sub-pixel pupils and teeth

- **Effort**: S · **Risk**: LOW · **Fix**: inflate `boundingSphere.radius` by ~24

### [P-3] `recolorWater` re-samples 6,561 vertices in one frame every ~2s of travel

## 8. DESIGN TENSIONS (gameplay findings — the fun ceiling)

*(Full mechanics map with exact formulas lives in the 2026-07-31 chat report;
these are the load-bearing conclusions, all arithmetic-verified.)*

- **[DT-1] Static 15s collect clock in a world that ramps forever** — the title
  mechanic peaks in the first 15 seconds and decays toward irrelevance (reach
  grows 2.2×; food density constant). `constants.js:76,11-12,163-164`.
- **[DT-2] Combo arithmetically unreachable at 1×** — 1.45s spawn latency + 35-50u
  spawn distance vs 4.5 u/s closing speed; a whole subsystem pays out only at high
  multipliers. `constants.js:18-19,30,27,156-157`.
- **[DT-3] Speed multiplier = difficulty dial + accessibility setting + unlabeled
  10× leaderboard multiplier** on one shared distance-ranked board that stores no
  multiplier (mobile's 1.75× likewise). `game.js:653-670`, `hiscores.js:26,57`.
- **[DT-4] Hunting suspends its own prey supply** — the band rotation (added to fix
  "I never get to eat anybody") gates on total population; every kill nets +1
  always-giant. Camp-and-hunt starves itself. `enemies.js:551,432-435,575`.
- **[DT-5] Enemies cannot catch a moving player** — 2.4 u/s max vs 6-13.2; the
  chase/vignette/heartbeat sell a threat kinematics can't deliver. Partly a
  recorded owner decision ("doubling the player must NOT speed enemies up") — the
  tension is that the feedback layer was built as if it weren't.
- **[DT-6] Jump is free, uncapped, and out-scales the only obstacle that pushes
  back** (rocks); water and enemy-dodging are correctly scoped out.
  `game.js:427-438,306-314`, `constants.js:205-208`.
- **[DT-7] Size stops paying at scale 7.67 (67th food) but never stops costing**
  (collider linear forever, camera further, giants track 1.5×you). Instrument,
  then decide. `constants.js:11-12,127`.
- **[DT-8] The curve goes flat at 1800u** — population caps at 1200u, speed at
  1800u, height self-cancels, every reward constant flat. An endless mode with no
  late game. `constants.js:154,162,160`, and reward constants `:6,7,20,177`.
- **[DT-9] Warn rings are seconds while hazards are units** — at 5× the telegraph's
  protection collapses (28.5u covered during a 0.95s warn). `constants.js:30,156-157`.
- **[DT-10] Everything celebrates acquisition; nothing celebrates survival** —
  `runTime` is surfaced only to tests; escapes/near-misses/biome crossings/ramp
  levels have zero acknowledgment; the dread channel has no positive resolution.
